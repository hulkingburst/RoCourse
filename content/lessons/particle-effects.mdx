A game without effects is a movie without color grading — technically functional, but flat. ParticleEmitters turn a plain part into a fountain of sparkles, a cloud of smoke, or a shower of coins. They're one of the fastest ways to make a game feel polished.

<Step>

## Your first emitter

A ParticleEmitter is an object you parent to a part. It spews particles automatically:

```luau
local emitter = Instance.new("ParticleEmitter")
emitter.Rate = 20          -- particles per second
emitter.Lifetime = NumberRange.new(0.5, 1.5)  -- each particle lives 0.5–1.5s
emitter.Speed = NumberRange.new(5, 10)         -- speed when emitted
emitter.Parent = part
```

- `Rate` — how many particles per second. Higher = denser effect.
- `Lifetime` — how long each particle lives (randomized between the two numbers).
- `Speed` — how fast particles fly outward.

## Shape and direction

By default, particles spray upward from the part's surface. Change where they go:

```luau
emitter.SpreadAngle = Vector2.new(45, 45)  -- spread in a cone
emitter.EmissionDirection = Enum.NormalId.Top  -- emit from the top face
```

`SpreadAngle` widens the spray cone — `Vector2.new(0, 0)` means a straight line, `Vector2.new(360, 360)` means everywhere.

For a coin pickup, you want particles flying outward from the center. For a torch, you want them rising from the top. Match the shape to the moment.

</Step>

## Color and size over time

The real magic: particles can change as they age.

```luau
emitter.Color = ColorSequence.new({
    ColorSequenceKeypoint.new(0, Color3.fromRGB(255, 215, 0)),  -- gold at start
    ColorSequenceKeypoint.new(1, Color3.fromRGB(255, 100, 0)),  -- orange at end
})
emitter.Size = NumberSequence.new({
    NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(0, 0.5),  -- start small
    NumberSequenceKeypoint.new(1, 0),    -- shrink to nothing
})
```

- `Color` — a ColorSequence: what color at the start (0) and end (1).
- `Size` — a NumberSequence: what size at the start and end.

These let you make sparkles that fade from gold to transparent, smoke that shrinks as it rises, or coins that shrink as they fly up.

## Toggling effects

Enable and disable emitters from a script:

```luau
emitter.Enabled = false  -- stop emitting (existing particles finish)
emitter.Enabled = true   -- start again
```

This is how most game effects work — the emitter sits on the part, disabled by default, and a script flips it on at the right moment (coin pickup, explosion, level-up).

## Common mistakes

- **Rate too high.** Hundreds of particles per second tank performance on mobile. Start low (10–30) and test on weaker devices.
- **Forgetting Lifetime.** Particles that live 30 seconds pile up and never leave. Keep pickup effects under 2 seconds.
- **Using Particles for UI.** ParticleEmitters are 3D world effects. For on-screen sparkles, use ImageLabels or Roact-based effects.
- **Not parenting the emitter.** An emitter with no parent emits nothing. Parent it to the part.
