You know the three windows you'll live in. This lesson is the rest of the building: the toolbar across the top, the 3D view in the middle, and the freedom to arrange everything around them. Studio is a big app — the fastest way to stop feeling lost in it is to learn how you move.

<Step>

## The toolbar — three zones

Every tool in Studio lives on the **toolbar** across the top, and it splits into three zones:

- **Left — testing.** The Play and Stop buttons sit here, within easy reach, because you'll press them constantly.
- **Middle — tabs.** **Home**, **Model**, **Avatar**, **UI**, **Script**, and **Plugins**. Clicking a tab swaps the row of tools below it. **Home** holds the everyday building tools — insert a part, the move, scale, and rotate tools, color and material, and the anchor toggle — so you'll spend most of your time on that tab.
- **Right — you.** Sharing, collaboration, Assistant, notifications, and your account.

<Note>
If a tool isn't on the toolbar, it's in a menu. The menus at the top are the full index of what Studio can do.
</Note>

## The 3D view is a camera

The big view in the middle isn't just a picture — it's a camera you fly around the world:

- **Hold the right mouse button and drag** to look around.
- While the right button is held: **WASD** moves (forward, left, back, right), **Q** and **E** move down and up, and **Shift** makes the camera go faster.
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out, and **hold the middle mouse button and drag** to pan sideways.
- With a part selected, press **F** to focus the camera on it.

That last one is the rescue button: whenever you get turned around or stranded in empty space, select something in the Explorer and press F to fly straight back to it.

## The windows are yours to arrange

The windows around the edges aren't nailed down. Drag one by its header bar and Studio reveals drop zones:

- Drop a window on an **edge** of another window to dock it along that edge.
- Drop it on the **center** of another window to group the two as tabs.
- Drag it away from every drop zone to let it **float** wherever you like — then drag it back to redock.
- Click a window's **collapse** button to pin it to the edge. It shrinks to a tab you can open with one click, and it tucks itself away again when you click somewhere else.

<Warning>
Rearranging panels is satisfying and easy to overdo. Set things up once so they feel comfortable, then get back to building.
</Warning>

And if something disappears, don't panic: every window is listed in the top menus — in current Studio that's the **Window** menu; if your version shows **View** instead, it's the same list. Tick the name and the window is back.

## When the interface changes, read the tool, not the spot

Roblox reshuffles Studio's interface every so often: buttons move, tabs get renamed, panels change shape. A tutorial video from a year ago shows a slightly different Studio than yours — and soon, yours will be the older one.

The map underneath never changes:

- **Tools at the top**
- **The world in the middle**
- **Windows around the edges**
- **Everything findable from the menus**

If you can't find a tool, hover the buttons — every tool shows a tooltip with its name and its shortcut. Read the tool, not the spot.
