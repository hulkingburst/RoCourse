The world's bugs are mostly about *timing* and *who runs what*: grabbing a character before it spawns, building a tween and never playing it, and playing sounds on the wrong computer. Three smells, five hunts.

<Step>

## Hunt one — the too-early character

A player joins and your script instantly errors on `player.Character.Humanoid` — nil.

```luau
Players.PlayerAdded:Connect(function(player)
    local humanoid = player.Character.Humanoid
end)
```

<Mcq
  question="Why is player.Character nil at join time?"
  options={[
    "The character spawns a moment after the player joins — you must listen for CharacterAdded",
    "Characters can't have Humanoids",
    "The player is invisible",
    "player.Character is a string",
  ]}
  answer={0}
  explanation="At join time the body doesn't exist yet. Listen for CharacterAdded and grab the character in its callback."
  label="Hunt one"
/>

</Step>

<Step>

## Hunt two — type the fix

Even inside CharacterAdded, the character's parts load over a few frames. Make the grab safe so it can't error on an unloaded part.

<FixBug
  instruction="Write the line that grabs the Humanoid safely."
  fix={["local humanoid = character:WaitForChild(\"Humanoid\")"]}
  explanation="WaitForChild blocks until the child exists, then returns it. character.Humanoid can error if it hasn't loaded."
  placeholder="local humanoid = ..."
  label="Hunt two"
>

```luau
local humanoid = character.Humanoid
```

</FixBug>

</Step>

<Step>

## Hunt three — the statue tween

You built the tween perfectly — TweenInfo, goals, Create — and the part sits there. No error, no motion.

<FixBug
  instruction="Write the missing line that starts the tween."
  fix={["tween:Play()"]}
  placeholder="tween:Play()"
  explanation="TweenService:Create makes the animation; :Play() runs it. Forgetting Play is the classic silent no-op."
  label="Hunt three"
>

```luau
local info = TweenInfo.new(0.5, Enum.EasingStyle.Quad, Enum.EasingDirection.Out)
local goals = { CFrame = coin.CFrame + Vector3.new(0, 5, 0) }

local tween = TweenService:Create(coin, info, goals)
```

</FixBug>

</Step>

<Step>

## Hunt four — the silent coin

You click the coin, the coins go up, but there's no sound — on anyone's computer.

```luau
-- Script (server)
coin.ClickDetector.MouseClick:Connect(function(player)
    coin.Sound:Play()
    clickEvent:FireServer()
end)
```

<Mcq
  question="The pickup sound plays nowhere. What's the likely cause?"
  options={[
    "The sound is played on the server — feel-effects belong on the client, per player",
    "The SoundId is too short",
    "Sounds need Looped = true",
    "Volume can't be set",
  ]}
  answer={0}
  explanation="A server :Play() is silent to the player who triggered it. Play feel-effects in a LocalScript where the player can hear them."
  label="Hunt four"
/>

</Step>

<Step>

## Hunt five — type the fix

You want the pickup sound to play for the clicking player, right before the reward is reported. Write the line that plays it.

<FixBug
  instruction="Write the line that plays the coin's sound on the client."
  fix={["coin.Sound:Play()"]}
  explanation="coin.Sound:Play() starts the sound where the player will actually hear it."
  placeholder="coin...:Play()"
  label="Hunt five"
>

```luau
coin.ClickDetector.MouseClick:Connect(function()
    -- play the pickup sound here
    clickEvent:FireServer()
end)
```

</FixBug>

</Step>

## Hunt six — arrange the pickup

Assemble a coin pickup: a touch, a character, a player, a reward.

<ArrangeCode
  lines={[
    "coin.Touched:Connect(function(other)",
    "    local character = other.Parent",

    "    end",
    "end)",

  distractors={[
    "player.leaderstats.Coins.Value = 999999",
