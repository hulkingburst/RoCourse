You've built a lot from scratch. But Roblox ships with ready-made objects for the most common jobs — spawning players, protecting them, and making parts stand out. Knowing these built-ins saves time and makes your game feel finished with a fraction of the code.

<Step>

## SpawnLocation — where players appear

A **SpawnLocation** is a part that teleports a player to it when they spawn. Place it on your starter platform and you no longer manage respawn positions by hand.

```luau
local spawnLocation = Instance.new("SpawnLocation")
spawnLocation.Position = Vector3.new(0, 5, 0)
spawnLocation.Neutral = false      -- team-colored, spawns any team
spawnLocation.Duration = 3         -- safety timer after respawn
spawnLocation.Parent = workspace
```

- `Neutral` — `true` means any team spawns there; `false` colors it to the spawn's team.
- `Duration` — helps pair with ForceField (below) so the player isn't shot the instant they appear.

The engine auto-detects SpawnLocations — you don't even need a script for basic respawning. Players just appear at the nearest enabled one.

## ForceField — a bubble of protection

A **ForceField** wraps a character in a translucent bubble that blocks damage. Perfect for the brief grace period after spawning.

```luau
local forceField = Instance.new("ForceField")
forceField.Visible = true
forceField.Parent = player.Character
```

Attach it to the character (or a spawned part) and every hit is ignored until you remove it.

```luau
task.wait(5)
forceField:Destroy()   -- protection ends
```

<Warning>
A ForceField only belongs on a freshly-spawned character. Leave it on forever and the player can't be hurt at all — a cheap trick that breaks combat and leaderboard deaths.
</Warning>

## SelectionBox and Highlight — making parts stand out

When a player needs to *notice* an object — a clickable crate, a quest item — add an outline. Two built-ins do this.

**SelectionBox** outlines a part with a wireframe box:

```luau
local box = Instance.new("SelectionBox")
box.Transparency = 0.6
box.Color3 = Color3.fromRGB(255, 215, 0)
box.Adornee = crate     -- the part to outline
box.Parent = crate
```

**Highlight** fills the whole part with a semi-transparent glow instead:

```luau
local glow = Instance.new("Highlight")
glow.FillTransparency = 0.5
glow.OutlineColor = Color3.fromRGB(255, 215, 0)
glow.Adornee = crate
glow.Parent = crate
```

SelectionBox for crisp outlines, Highlight for a softer glow — pick whichever reads better for the moment.

## Combining them — a protected respawn

The pattern that ties it together. When a player spawns, they appear at the SpawnLocation inside a temporary ForceField:

```luau
local Players = game:GetService("Players")

Players.PlayerAdded:Connect(function(player)
    player.CharacterAdded:Connect(function(character)
        local forceField = Instance.new("ForceField")
        forceField.Parent = character
        task.wait(3)
        forceField:Destroy()
    end)
end)
```

## Common mistakes

- **Building a respawn system from scratch.** SpawnLocation handles it. Reach for the built-in before writing custom teleport logic.
- **Leaving ForceField on forever.** Timing or a `:Destroy()` ends protection; a permanent bubble is a bug, not a feature.
- **Forgetting `Adornee`.** SelectionBox and Highlight do nothing until you point them at a part.
- **Using these on the server.** SpawnLocation and ForceField work fine there, but Highlight/SelectionBox visuals are often best kept minimal and performance-aware — too many glows tank frame rate on mobile.
